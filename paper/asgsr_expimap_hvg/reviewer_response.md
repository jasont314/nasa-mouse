## Remaining limitations

1. The project-held-out analysis reuses OSDR and is not an independent mission cohort. A new flight cohort is still needed for external validation.
2. Tabula Muris Senis markers provide broad composition proxies, not quantitative deconvolution. The atlas also lacks mature myofibers, limiting soleus adjustment.
3. Three seeds expose important optimization sensitivity but do not fully characterize model uncertainty.
4. The tissue selection, original pathway review, and literature-derived labels remain retrospective. The integrated evidence matrix cannot make the discovery phase prespecified after the fact.
5. The five-check status evaluates directional reproducibility, not multiplicity-controlled statistical significance or causality. Kidney illustrates this distinction because all three retained programs pass five directional checks but have GSEA FDR above 0.05.
6. The primary spleen result excludes OSD-288 for recorded condition-strain disjointness; this improves the contrast but reduces the number of independent projects to five.

These limitations are stated in the manuscript and are the appropriate boundary for the current ASGSR submission.
